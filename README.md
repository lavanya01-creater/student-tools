# student-tools
free list of tools and tips to learn english and study smarter
## 🗣️ Learn English (Free)

- [Duolingo](https://www.duolingo.com) – Fun daily lessons, great for beginners
- [BBC Learning English](https://www.bbc.co.uk/learningenglish) – Free lessons, videos, and grammar
- [Cambridge Dictionary](https://dictionary.cambridge.org) – Best free dictionary with example sentences
- [YouGlish](https://youglish.com) – Hear any word pronounced in real YouTube videos

## 🛠️ Free Tools for Students

- [Google Docs](https://docs.google.com) – Free writing and document tool
- [Grammarly](https://www.grammarly.com) – Checks your grammar and spelling
- [Notion](https://www.notion.so) – Organize notes, homework, and schedules
- [Canva](https://www.canva.com) – Make free presentations and posters

## 💡 Study Tips

- Study in short 25-minute sessions with 5-minute breaks (Pomodoro method)
- Watch English videos with subtitles — turn them off once it gets easier
- Practice speaking out loud daily, even alone
